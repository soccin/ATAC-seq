Subject: Results for Project {PROJNO} ATACseq ready

The output for the ATACseq pipeline ({VERSION}) is now ready. You can access it at:

   https://bicdelivery.mskcc.org/project/{PROJNO}/atacseq/{RUNDIR}

The ATACseq specific results are in the folder

   atacSeq

Where you will find the following subfolders:

- bigwig: These are the coverage profiles in bigwig format, scaled to 10 million reads. Can be loaded into IGV to get a figure of the coverage.

- macs: the output from the MACS2 peak calling program, one folder per sample. The *.blacklistFiltered.* files are the same peaks with those in ENCODE blacklisted regions removed; use these for any analysis of a sample's peaks.

- atlas: the merged list of peaks found in all samples (macsPeaksMerged.saf) and the raw count matrix over it (peaks_raw_fcCounts.txt), the input for any normalization or differential analysis.

- metrics: Plots of various QC metrics for the samples.

   - Proj_{PROJNO}__postInsDistribution.pdf: simple fragment length distribution. There should be some signature of nucleosomal periodicity.

   - Proj_{PROJNO}__ATACSeqQC.pdf

      - Plots of the number and percentage of each sample's fragments in peaks.

      - Sequencing depth in peaks; samples below half the median are marked LOW DEPTH.

      - PCA from the peak atlas. Can be used to look for potential outliers. A second PCA, with every sample downsampled to the same depth, shows whether an outlier is one only because of its depth.

   - Proj_{PROJNO}__ATACSeqDepth.csv: the depth numbers of the QC report, one row per sample.

   - <sample>_postProcess.tss_enrich.csv: TSS enrichment score per sample. The tss_enrich column is the key QC metric; ENCODE considers values above 7 ideal for hg38 (above 10 for b37, above 15 for mm10). The plots tss_enrich.pdf and large_tss_enrich.png show the read coverage centered on transcription start sites.

   - <sample>_postProcess___MITO.txt: the fraction of reads on the mitochondrial genome. Mitochondrial reads are left out of the peaks, the atlas and the coverage profiles.

   - <sample>_postProcess___INS.txt and ___INS.pdf: Picard's insert size metrics and histogram per sample.
