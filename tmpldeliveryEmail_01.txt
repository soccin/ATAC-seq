Subject: Results for Project {PROJNO} ATACseq ready

The results of the ATAC-seq pipeline ({VERSION}) are ready at:

   https://bicdelivery.mskcc.org/project/{PROJNO}/atacseq/{RUNDIR}

The main files, in the folder atacSeq:

- atlas/peaks_raw_fcCounts.txt: the raw counts of each sample in each
  region of the merged peak atlas
- macs: the peaks of each sample
- bigwig: a signal track per sample, for a genome browser
- metrics/Proj_{PROJNO}__ATACSeqQC.pdf: the QC report
