Subject: Results for Project {PROJNO} ATACseq ready

The output of the ATAC-seq pipeline ({VERSION}) is now ready. You can
access it at:

   https://bicdelivery.mskcc.org/project/{PROJNO}/atacseq/{RUNDIR}

The outputs and the methods are documented online:

   https://github.com/soccin/ATAC-seq/blob/{VERSION}/docs/output.md
   https://github.com/soccin/ATAC-seq/blob/{VERSION}/docs/methods.md

PDF copies are in the folder atacSeq/docs.

Read filtering, the Tn5 shift and peak calling follow the ENCODE
ATAC-seq pipeline, with four differences: reads are kept at a mapping
quality of {MAPQ} or more; duplicates are marked when the reads are
mapped, before the filter; reads whose 5' end is soft-clipped are
removed, with their mates; and pairs with an insert of 30 bp or less
are removed. The last two remove short partial alignments of reads
that are not from the reference genome. Mitochondrial reads and
contigs other than the autosomes, X and Y are left out of the peaks,
the atlas and the signal tracks.

The results are in the folder atacSeq, which has these subfolders:

- macs: the peaks of each sample, one folder per sample.

   - *_peaks.narrowPeak, *_summits.bed, *_peaks.xls: MACS2's output,
     as MACS2 wrote it.

   - *_peaks.blacklistFiltered.narrowPeak and
     *_summits.blacklistFiltered.bed: the same peaks after ENCODE's
     steps: at most the 300,000 most significant, clipped to the
     chromosome ends, without the peaks that overlap the ENCODE
     blacklist. Use these for any analysis of a sample's peaks.

- atlas: the merged peak atlas and the counts over it.

   - macsPeaksMerged.saf: the blacklist-filtered peaks of all samples,
     merged within 500 bp, in SAF format (1-based coordinates).

   - peaks_raw_fcCounts.txt: the raw number of fragments of each
     sample in each atlas region. Use it for any normalization or
     differential analysis.

   - peaks_raw_fcCounts.txt.summary: for each sample, the fragments
     in the atlas (Assigned) and outside it.

- bigwig: one coverage track per sample, scaled to 10 million reads,
  for IGV or the UCSC Genome Browser.

- metrics: QC metrics and plots.

   - Proj_{PROJNO}__ATACSeqQC.pdf: the fraction and number of
     each sample's fragments in the atlas (computed on the atlas of
     all samples, so not ENCODE's FRiP); each sample's depth in peaks,
     with a sample below half the median marked LOW DEPTH; a PCA of
     the samples, with point size by depth; and the same PCA after
     every sample is thinned at random to the depth of the smallest.
     A sample apart from its group in the first PCA but not in the
     second is apart because of its depth.

   - Proj_{PROJNO}__ATACSeqDepth.csv: the depth numbers of
     the QC report, one row per sample.

   - Proj_{PROJNO}__postInsDistribution.pdf: the fragment
     length distribution of each sample, 31 to 1000 bp. A good
     library shows a nucleosome-free peak below about 100 bp and
     nucleosome peaks at about 200 and 400 bp.

   - <sample>_postProcess___INS.txt and ___INS.pdf: Picard's insert
     size metrics and histogram for each sample.

   - <sample>_postProcess___MITO.txt: the fraction of each sample's
     reads on the mitochondrial genome, counted after duplicates are
     removed, so lower than the fraction ENCODE reports.

   - <sample>_postProcess.tss_enrich.csv: the ENCODE TSS enrichment
     score (column tss_enrich), with two plots, tss_enrich.pdf and
     large_tss_enrich.png. ENCODE's guide depends on the genome:
     hg38 acceptable 5 to 7, ideal above 7; b37 acceptable 6 to 10,
     ideal above 10; mm10 acceptable 10 to 15, ideal above 15.

- docs: output.pdf and methods.pdf, the two documents linked above.
